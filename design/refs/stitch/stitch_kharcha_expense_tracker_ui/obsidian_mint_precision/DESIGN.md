## Brand & Style

This design system targets financially disciplined, design-conscious professionals navigating personal and business capital in India. The visual language conveys uncompromising technical precision, sovereign control, and modern prestige. By synthesising dark-mode minimalism with tactical fintech density, the interface transforms high-frequency tracking into an effortless, tactile ritual.

The aesthetic fuses **Precision Fintech Minimalism** with **Android 15 Material 3 Expressive**. Key characteristics include:
- Deep architectural obsidian bases that preserve OLED battery and reduce eye fatigue.
- Deliberate semantic color hits signaling transactional movement without visual clutter.
- Edge-to-edge structural discipline with razor-sharp 1px structural framing, high-density transactional ledgers, and expressive pill-formed dynamic affordances.
- Unwavering respect for Indian currency conventions (lakh and crore decimal grouping) rendered via tabular monospaced numeric forms.

## Colors

The palette operates on calibrated contrast ratios strictly tuned for an obsidian dark canvas. 

### Surface Tiers
- **Canvas Base (`#0C0E12`)**: True deep obsidian; underpins the entire screen canvas and system navigation bars.
- **Surface Level 1 (`#161920`)**: Recessed cards, ledger backings, and list item containers.
- **Surface Level 2 (`#202530`)**: Elevated sheets, modal drawers, dynamic filters, and active states.
- **Structural Stroke (`#2D3340`)**: Rigid 1px hairline border tone used to delineate bounding surfaces and tabular rows.

### Accent & Semantic Roles
- **Primary Teal (`#2DD4BF`)**: High-visibility mint used for active brand touches, key CTAs, selected toggles, and hero summary focal points.
- **Secondary Teal (`#0F766E`)**: Deep companion shade for filled container backgrounds, active tracking badges, and subtle icon substrates.
- **Turmeric Gold (`#F59E0B`)**: Dedicated highlight for actionable investments, pending settlements, tax allocations, and high-priority tags.
- **Credit Emerald (`#10B981`)**: Signals inflows, refunds, returns, and positive balances.
- **Debit Crimson (`#F43F5E`)**: Signals expense deductions, bills, and negative net flow.
- **Text & Foreground**: Full bright `#F8FAFC` for headline data, `#94A3B8` for secondary labels, and `#475569` for disabled states and hairline structural dividers.

## Typography

The typographic system separates qualitative editorial labels from quantitative transaction metrics.

- **Primary Grotesk (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Delivers geometric clarity and warm contemporary authority for navigational headlines, category titles, metadata, and interface actions.
- **Monospaced Numerics (`JetBrains Mono`)**: Mandated for all Indian Rupee amounts, timestamps, UPI transaction IDs, account codes, and tabular metrics. Monospace spacing ensures numbers stack with vertical precision across decimal points without baseline jitter.
- **Currency Presentation**: Render Indian Rupee metrics adhering to standard subcontinent numeric phrasing (e.g., `₹12,45,678.50`). The currency symbol (`₹`) is weighted slightly lighter than the numerals to maintain optical balance.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is anchored in native Android 15 edge-to-edge geometry, operating on a fluid 4-column structure on mobile devices, reflowing into an 8-column system on compact tablets and a 12-column grid on desktop/foldable dashboards.

### Responsive Behaviors
- **Mobile (<600dp)**: 4 columns, 16px margins, 12px gutters. Primary actions sit anchored within bottom thumb navigation zones. Edge-to-edge transparent system bars push content beneath the navigation scrim.
- **Tablet / Foldable (600dp - 1024dp)**: 8 columns, 24px margins, 16px gutters. Dashboard transforms into split structural panes (ledger on left, contextual detail card on right).
- **Desktop / Expansive (>1024dp)**: 12 columns, 40px margins, 24px gutters. Center-confined dashboard with a maximum operational content width of 1280px.

### Density and Safe Insets
The spacing scale prioritizes information density. Micro-gaps (`space-xs` and `space-sm`) bind label-to-metric pairings, while structural gutters maintain separation between distinct bank accounts and categorical groupings. All bottom sheets and full-canvas views integrate dynamic Android `WindowInsets` to respect navigation bars, home gestural indicators, and display cutouts.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids heavy drop shadows in favor of **structural tonal layering** combined with **1px hairline strokes**. In deep-black environments, structural borders provide superior visual grounding without muddying contrast.

- **Base Layer (Elevation 0)**: Unadorned `#0C0E12`.
- **Card Tier (Elevation 1)**: `#161920` surface with a crisp `1px solid #2D3340` border. Zero drop shadow.
- **Floating Controls & Modals (Elevation 2)**: `#202530` backdrop, `1px solid #2D3340` border, reinforced by a directional micro-glow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`.
- **Active Accented Surfaces**: Surfaces featuring active financial updates gain a subtle localized under-glow: `0 0 16px -4px rgba(45, 212, 191, 0.15)`.
- **Overlay Modals**: Utilize an explicit backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`) over a `#0C0E12CC` scrim.

## Shapes

The design system implements a controlled shape hierarchy balancing analytical rigor with Material 3 Expressive ergonomics:

- **Containers & Master Cards**: Styled with `rounded-lg` (16px / 1rem) for an approachable, self-contained surface feel.
- **Compact Elements, Inputs & List Items**: Styled with base roundedness (8px / 0.5rem) to maximize internal screen real estate and retain dense data alignment.
- **Indicators, Chips, & Primary Action Pills**: Fully pill-shaped (`rounded-full` / 9999px) to establish clear interactive affordance against rectangular card backgrounds.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA**: Pill-shaped container (`rounded-full`), solid `#2DD4BF` fill, `#0C0E12` bold typography. Height is fixed at 48px to meet Android touch targets.
- **Secondary Action**: Translucent container (`#2DD4BF1A`), 1px stroke in `#2DD4BF4D`, `#2DD4BF` typography.
- **Destructive Action**: `#F43F5E1A` background with `#F43F5E` label and stroke.

### Chips & Filter Indicators
- Scaled to a compact 32px height using a pill shape.
- Unselected: Surface `#161920` with `#2D3340` border, `#94A3B8` text.
- Selected: Surface `#0F766E33` with `#2DD4BF` 1px border and `#2DD4BF` label.
- Category pills feature a dedicated 6px circular dot indicator (e.g., `#F59E0B` for Investments, `#10B981` for Salary).

### Expense & Credit Transaction Lists
- Grouped inside an Elevation 1 (`#161920`) card container.
- Individual transaction rows feature a 40px rounded icon container, semantic category glyph, bold title in `body-md`, and date/account sublabel in `body-sm`.
- Trailing metrics display amounts in `amount-md`: Emerald Green (`+#10B981`) for credits and clean high-contrast White (`-#F8FAFC`) with Crimson subtext for debits.
- Rows are separated by a 1px `#2D3340` divider inset to the text baseline.

### Input Fields & Keypads
- Grounded `#161920` background with a mandatory 1px `#2D3340` boundary; transitions to `#2DD4BF` on focus.
- Dedicated numeric input field displays a trailing fixed `₹` symbol in muted slate, while the user value populates in monospaced `amount-hero` sizing.

### Cards & Metrics Summaries
- **Hero Cashflow Card**: Uses `#161920` surface with an accent-tinted top edge border. Displays net balance, period delta pill (`+14.2%`), and side-by-side debit/credit mini-indicators.
- **Bank & UPI Tile**: Compact horizontally scrollable pill cards showing linked account avatars, masked identifier (`•• 4821`), and active balance in `amount-sm`.
